Pounded Yam and Egusi Soup

Description

This is a rich, savory Nigerian soup thickened with ground melon seeds (egusi) and loaded with leafy greens and assorted meats. It is traditionally paired with pounded yam or another "swallow."

Ingredients

  • Egusi Base: 200g ground egusi seeds, 1 medium onion, 2 scotch bonnet peppers, and 4 plum tomatoes (blended together)
  • Proteins: 500g assorted meats (beef, tripe, or goat meat) and 1 piece of dried fish (smoked panla or catfish)
  • Greens & Aromatics: 1 cup chopped spinach or ugu (fluted pumpkin leaves), 1/2 cup palm oil, 2 tablespoons ground crayfish, and stock cubes/salt to taste

Steps

  1. Boil the Meats: Cook your assorted meats with seasoned chopped onions and stock cubes until tender. Save the meat stock.
  2. Make the Egusi Paste: Mix the ground egusi with a little warm water or blended onion to form a thick paste.
  3. Fry the Base: Heat palm oil in a pot for 2 minutes. Add a handful of chopped onions, then add the egusi paste in small chunks. Fry on medium heat for about 10 minutes, stirring gently so it forms small lumps.
  4. Simmer with Stock: Pour in the blended tomato and pepper mix, followed by the meat stock and dried fish. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. Add Greens: Stir in the cooked meats, ground crayfish, and season to taste. Add the chopped greens and cook for another 3–5 minutes before turning off the heat
