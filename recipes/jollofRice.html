Jollof-Rice

Description

Jollof Rice is a popular West African one-pot meal made by simmering long-grain rice in a rich, spiced tomato and red pepper sauce.

Ingredients

  • Rice: 500g long-grain parboiled rice (or golden sella basmati)
  • Pepper & Tomato Base: 4 medium plum tomatoes, 2 red bell peppers (tatashe), 1-2 scotch bonnet chillies (ata rodo), and 2 medium onions
  • Aromatics & Seasoning: 3 tablespoons tomato paste, 1/2 cup vegetable oil, 2 bay leaves, 1 teaspoon curry powder, 1 teaspoon dried thyme, and stock cubes (or bouillon) with salt to taste
  • Liquid: 500ml chicken or beef stoc

Steps

  1. Blend the Base: Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, scotch bonnets, and one of the onions together until smooth.
  2. Fry the Stew Base: Heat the vegetable oil in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add finely diced onion and fry until soft, then stir in the tomato paste and fry for about 3-5 minutes until it darkens. Pour in the blended pepper-tomato mix, curry powder, thyme, and bay leaves. Cook for 15–20 minutes until the sauce reduces and the oil separates on top.
  3. Add Stock: Stir in the chicken or beef stock and season with salt and stock cubes. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer.
  4. Add Rice: Wash the parboiled rice thoroughly under cold water until the water runs clear. Stir the drained rice into the simmering pot so every grain is coated.
  5. Steam to Perfection: Cover the pot tightly with a layer of aluminum foil and a tight-fitting lid to trap the steam. Turn the heat down to low and cook for about 20-25 minutes without opening the lid.
  6. Create the Smoky Crust (Party Style): Increase the heat slightly for the last few minutes to lightly toast the bottom of the rice for a traditional smoky flavor. Remove from heat, let it rest for 10 minutes, and fluff gently with a fork.
  7. Serve: Pair your jollof rice with fried plantains, coleslaw, or grilled chicken.
